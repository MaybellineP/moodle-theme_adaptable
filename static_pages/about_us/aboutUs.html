About Us

At PSI, we believe that quality healthcare should be accessible to everyone. PharmaLink bridges gaps in the healthcare system by integrating pharmacy services with primary care, ensuring that communities receive the support they need, when they need it.
Millions of people struggle with fragmented healthcare services, including immunization, making it difficult to access essential medications and vaccines, consultations, and preventative care. PharmaLink was created to connect these missing links, brining innovative, patient-centered care to the forefront.
  • Seamless Integration: We connect pharmacies, healthcare providers, and partners in a unified system backed by data
  • Convenience: Patients access essential health services, including vaccination, from nearby pharmacy
  • Improved Health Outcomes: Timely interventions and better coordination lead to healthier communities

Our Team

Betty Abera
Project Director II, Global

Betty Abera is a global health specialist and registered pharmacist with over twenty years of experience in expanding access to medicines and vaccines through innovative healthcare delivery models and strategic partnerships.

Betty is the Global Project Director for PharmaLink at PSI Washington, DC, where she leads the introduction of Pharmacy-Based Immunization Delivery in Ethiopia, Nigeria, and Kenya through effective stakeholder engagement, policy advocacy, and resource mobilization. Betty designed a comprehensive framework for concept to delivery of pharmacy-based immunization, focusing on expanding the enabling environment, strengthening pharmacist capacity, developing a digital ecosystem for eLearning, community and data management, fostering public trust and demand generation, creating a sustainable financial model, and generating evidence to support scalability. Betty also collaborates with the Viya Health (Social Business) team to drive product pipeline innovation.

Prior to her current role, Betty led social business marketing and product pipeline innovation at PSI Washington, DC, supporting local teams in over 20 countries across Africa, Asia and Latin America. She managed pharmaceutical marketing for GSK in East Africa and oversaw operations for Rite Aid pharmacy chains in Seattle, Washington, while also dedicating a decade to direct patient care. Betty earned her Bachelor of Science degrees in Chemistry and Pharmacy from the University of Albera, Canada and holds an MBA in International Business from the University of Greenwich, UK.
